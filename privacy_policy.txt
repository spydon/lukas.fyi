Privacy policy for Sun Tracker

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Sun Tracker is an Android app made by Lukas Klingsbo (spydon). It shows where
the sun is on the sky at your location. This policy explains what the app does
with information on your device.

In short: Sun Tracker does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any
personal information. Everything happens on your device.


No data leaves your device

Sun Tracker does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send
any information anywhere. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting and no accounts.


Camera

The camera is used only to show a live view behind the sun overlay. The app
does not take photos, record video or save any images, and the camera view is
never sent anywhere.


Location

Your location is used only to calculate where the sun is, when it rises and
sets, and the difference between magnetic and true north where you are. It is
kept in memory while the app is running and is never saved or sent anywhere.
The location is provided by the location services of your Android device, which
are covered by the privacy policy of their provider.


Motion sensors

The compass, accelerometer and gyroscope are used only to know which way the
phone is pointing. These readings are used immediately and never saved or sent
anywhere.


Settings stored on your device

The app saves its calibration settings (compass correction and field of view)
on your device so that they are kept between launches. These settings contain
no personal information and are deleted when you uninstall the app.


Permissions

You can deny or revoke the camera and location permissions at any time in the
Android settings. Without the camera the app shows the sun over a plain
background, and without location it cannot calculate where the sun is.


Children

Sun Tracker does not collect any information from anyone, including children.


Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at
https://spydon.dev/privacy_policy.txt with an updated date.


Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, open an issue at
https://github.com/spydon/sun_tracker/issues
